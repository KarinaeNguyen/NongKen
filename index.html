Preserving Vietnam's Silk Heritage

Born in Bac Ninh — cradle of UNESCO-recognised Quan Ho folk songs — Nong Kén safeguards the thousand-year silk-weaving tradition of Vong Nguyet village, transforming each thread into a meaningful story for the modern world.

Thousand-Year Tradition

Rooted in Vong Nguyet village's centuries-old weaving techniques, every thread carries a story passed down through generations.

Artisan-First Model

We work directly with master weavers, ensuring fair income and the continuation of a craft once at risk of disappearing forever.

Guaranteed Authenticity

QR-powered digital tags give every customer full traceability from cocoon to creation — a first for Vietnamese souvenirs.
