About Us

The inception of Nong Kén is deeply rooted in the rich cultural tapestry of Bac Ninh, a province renowned as the cradle of Quan Ho folk songs — a UNESCO-recognised Intangible Cultural Heritage — and home to a vast system of historic temples and pagodas. The project stems from an urgent need to safeguard the thousand-year-old silk-weaving tradition of Vong Nguyet village, which has faced severe challenges from post-war economic shifts and market globalisation.

Preliminary research into the Bac Ninh tourism market revealed a distinct gap: current souvenir offerings are predominantly limited to culinary specialties, leaving a void for premium, high-application products that embody the province's cultural essence for affluent travellers and citizens seeking meaningful gifts.

Operating within the high-end cultural tourism and souvenir industry, Nong Kén serves as a bridge between the historical craftsmanship of local artisans and the sophisticated demands of contemporary consumers. The brand name “Nong Kén” symbolises the nurturing of silk cocoons — a metaphor for preserving and incubating the delicate values of national identity in a modern era.

Unlike mass-produced souvenirs, Nong Kén distinguishes itself through Digital Transparency, utilising QR code technology to provide full traceability for every product. Each purchase is not just a transaction — it is a verified contribution to cultural preservation and a deliberate act of heritage stewardship.

Our Vision & Mission

Become the Definitive Gift Brand of Bac Ninh

Vision

To become the definitive, characteristic gift brand associated with Bac Ninh — recognised and mentioned first not only by international tourists but also by local residents throughout the region.

Mission
