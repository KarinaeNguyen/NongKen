Lotus Silk Scarf

Vong Nguyet Village, Bac Ninh, Vietnam

Crafted from premium silk by artisans of Vong Nguyet village, this Lotus Silk Scarf embodies the elegance of Bac Ninh’s cultural heritage. Its soft drape, luminous finish, and symbolic lotus motif make it ideal for both daily refinement and meaningful gifting.

Every piece includes QR-enabled traceability so customers can view weaving origin, artisan profile, and cultural story. By choosing Nong Kén, you directly support local craft families and help preserve a thousand-year weaving tradition for future generations.
